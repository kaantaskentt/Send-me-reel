# Research brief

Decide whether ContextDrop should be a standalone Mac app, a content-to-agent bridge, a narrowly focused resource finder, or a different product supported by the evidence. The prior recommendation is a hypothesis, not a constraint.

Investigate the job people already struggle to complete after seeing useful AI content. Separate saving, rediscovery, understanding, identifying a resource, evaluating relevance, setup and adaptation. Prefer recent first-person descriptions of behavior and existing workarounds. Do not infer willingness to pay from likes, comments, repository stars or a competitor's marketing.

Find the closest substitute for each job, including a general-purpose multimodal model and a simple skill. Ask what ContextDrop would do materially better, how the difference can be measured, and whether a customer would pay for that difference. Search deliberately for disconfirming evidence, satisfied users of current workflows, and products that already implement the proposed advantage.

Constrain the operating stack to two core paid AI vendors at most, plus an optional metered acquisition fallback only if its benefit is demonstrated. Prefer built-in/local tools and one durable local database. Include failed imports, retries, reasoning, storage, support and agent execution in unit-economics assumptions. Distinguish infrastructure cost, customer-paid provider usage and ContextDrop's margin.

Investigate recovery of one- and two-frame clues. Account for every decoded source frame and test whether the candidate detector and OCR recover the target, including motion, small text, low contrast, compression and absent targets. Do not substitute candidate retention for correct recognition or a synthetic demonstration for real platform success. Preserve exact source timestamps and originals; never invent hidden or unreadable content.

Evaluate Agent Reach from pinned implementation and documentation. Use relevant capabilities only when they improve a specific research or capture task. Installation success, a listed platform or a connected browser does not prove exact post acquisition or video understanding. Do not install broad tool bundles just to increase the tool count.

Produce an evidence ledger, a closest-substitute comparison, a transparent cost model, a stronger offline experiment, and a revised product/marketing decision. State what changed, what survived scrutiny, what remains unknown, and what observable result would cause the recommendation to change again.

Stop expanding desk research when the remaining important uncertainties require customer observation or a real integration test. Specify that experiment; do not pretend more search results settle it. No outreach, posting, account creation or paid tool subscription is authorized by this research brief.
